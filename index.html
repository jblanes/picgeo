GeoClick

A picture of a place somewhere on Earth. Press and hold on the globe where you think it is. Every kilometre you are off is added to your score. Ten rounds, from easy to very hard. Lowest score wins.

Four hints per game, each usable once: zoom to the country, the name of the place, the flag, or the exact spot. Once per game, after a bad round, you can cap it at 1,000 km.

Daily: everyone gets the same ten places today, so totals can be compared. Free play: random places you have not seen yet.

Map tiles stream from the internet and stay sharp when you zoom in.
